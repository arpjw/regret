\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced Regret, an open CFR$^+$ solver with a reproducible empirical
analysis framework. Our main findings: CFR$^+$ converges approximately an
order of magnitude faster than vanilla CFR in practice; abstraction error
from card bucketing decreases sharply up to $50$ buckets and slowly
thereafter; action-abstraction error is more sensitive to which sizes are
included than to set size; and CFR$^+$ can be formally understood as
Optimistic FTRL in self-play, explaining its faster convergence.

All results are reproducible via the Regret web interface.
