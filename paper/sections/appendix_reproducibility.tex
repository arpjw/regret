\section{Reproducibility checklist}
\label{app:reproducibility}

\subsection*{Hardware}
All experiments were run on a MacBook Pro (Apple M-series, 16\,GB RAM)
under macOS Darwin~25. Wall-clock numbers in the paper assume a single
performance core; Regret is currently single-threaded.

\subsection*{Software versions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Rust toolchain: \texttt{rustc 1.94+ (stable)}
  \item Node.js: $\geq 18$
  \item Regret commit hash: pinned at submission (see repository).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Random seeds}
Card-abstraction Monte Carlo rollouts use a deterministic seed
(\texttt{0x5EED} by default; \texttt{0xC0FFEE} for the experiments in
Sections~\ref{sec:convergence}--\ref{sec:abstraction-error}). CFR$^+$
updates are deterministic given the seed.

\subsection*{Reproducing the paper figures}
The full pipeline lives under \texttt{paper/experiments/}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{cargo build --release --bin run\_experiments}
  \item \texttt{./target/release/run\_experiments --out paper/experiments/expN.json --experiments N}
        (one of $N\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ per process; parallelizable across cores)
  \item \texttt{python3 paper/experiments/finalize.sh} --- merges the
        per-experiment JSONs into \texttt{results.json}, re-renders
        Figures~1--4 at 300\,DPI, and rewrites the prose tables in
        Sections~\ref{sec:convergence}--\ref{sec:abstraction-error}.
\end{itemize}
Wall-clock budget on the reference hardware: roughly $13$\,min for
experiment~1 at $T\!=\!1{,}000$, $115$\,min for experiment~2 (dominated
by $K\!=\!200$), $90$\,min for experiment~3 (dominated by $K\!=\!100\!\times\!3$ boards),
and $30$\,min for experiment~4 at $T\!=\!1{,}500$.
